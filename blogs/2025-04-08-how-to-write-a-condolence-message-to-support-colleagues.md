Supporting colleagues through challenging times fosters a positive workplace culture and strengthens professional relationships. Writing a thoughtful condolence message shows empathy and care, demonstrating that you value your colleagues beyond their professional roles. In this article, we’ll explore how to craft a heartfelt condolence message that strikes the right tone, when to send it, and why doing so can enhance your professional network and reputation.
